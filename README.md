# Microsoft Fabric Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Introduction

Built an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Fabric to transform raw online sales data into actionable business insights.

The project analyzes revenue, units sold, transactions, product categories, regional performance, payment methods, and individual product performance to provide a centralized view of sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.

The solution follows an end-to-end Microsoft Fabric workflow, from data ingestion and transformation to data modeling and interactive reporting.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Fabric
- Data Pipeline - Data ingestion
- Lakehouse - Data storage and processing
- Notebook - Data transformation and preparation
- Warehouse - Structured analytical storage
- Semantic Model - Data modeling and reporting layer
- Power BI Report - Interactive dashboard and visualization

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business lacks a centralized view of its sales performance, making it difficult to:

Track revenue and sales trends over time
Identify high-performing product categories
Compare regional sales performance
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Identify high-revenue products
Evaluate payment method preferences

The dashboard addresses these challenges by consolidating key sales metrics into a single interactive reporting solution, enabling management to identify revenue opportunities, product trends, and regional performance gaps.

## 📂 Data Source

Source: Kaggle Online Sales Dataset
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shreyanshverma27/online-sales-dataset-popular-marketplace-data

## 🔎 Key Business Questions Answered

- How does revenue and unit sales change month over month?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories have the highest number of units sold?
- Which region generates the highest revenue?
- Which region sells the highest number of units?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Which individual products generate the most revenue?
- Which products and categories represent potential revenue opportunities?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronics is the leading revenue category, generating $34,982, approximately 43.4% of total revenue.
- North America generates the highest regional revenue at approximately $36.8K, accounting for nearly 46% of total revenue.
- Asia leads in units sold with 233 units, representing approximately 45% of total units sold.
- March is the strongest revenue month, generating approximately $13K, after which monthly revenue shows a declining trend.
- Clothing leads in units sold with 145 units, while Electronics leads in revenue, highlighting a difference between sales volume and revenue contribution.
- Credit Card is the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 120 transactions (50%).
- The dashboard records 240 transactions generating $80.6K in revenue, with an average revenue of approximately $335.7 per transaction.

## 📈 Business Impact

The dashboard enables the business to:

- Prioritize high-value categories such as Electronics, which contributes approximately 43% of total revenue, for targeted inventory and promotional strategies.
- Identify regional opportunities by comparing revenue and sales volume, particularly the gap between Asia's high unit volume and lower revenue contribution.
- Monitor revenue trends and investigate the post-March decline to identify potential seasonal, pricing, or demand-related factors.
- Optimize product strategy by identifying high-revenue products and distinguishing between products that drive sales volume versus those that drive revenue.
- Improve customer strategy by analyzing payment preferences and transaction behavior to support more targeted promotions, payment optimization, and revenue-growth initiatives.
